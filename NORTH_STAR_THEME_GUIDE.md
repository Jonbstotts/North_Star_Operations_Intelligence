# North Star Operations Intelligence — Appearance

North Star is the permanent product identity. Appearance presets and seasonal themes only change interface colors and optional decorative effects; they never switch the product identity or runtime.

The current desktop-first design favors high-quality rendering. The former hardware performance profiles were removed in v1.2.0. Overlay density can still be adjusted with Low / Medium / High intensity, while severe-weather priority can suppress decorative effects immediately.
